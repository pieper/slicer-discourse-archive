## Post #1 by @Nick_Bearup (2026-10-15 18:44 UTC)

<p>Kitware is hosting a free webinar on <strong>Getting Started with 3D Slicer for Medical Imaging</strong> on <strong>Wednesday, October 21st, from 12 - 1 PM ET</strong>.</p>
<p>In this webinar, Kitware’s Sam Horvath and Ebrahim Ebrahim will walk through the fundamentals of 3D Slicer and demonstrate how it can be used for <strong>medical image segmentation</strong>. The session will cover:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Navigating 3D Slicer and loading medical imaging data</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Using the built-in Segment Editor tools</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Approaches for segmenting anatomical structures and regions of interest</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Popular AI-based segmentation tools</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A demonstration of the TotalSegmentator module</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The session is designed for <strong>researchers, developers, engineers, and clinical or medical users</strong> who are new to 3D Slicer or want to better understand its segmentation capabilities.</p>
<p>The event is free, but you do have to register beforehand:</p>
