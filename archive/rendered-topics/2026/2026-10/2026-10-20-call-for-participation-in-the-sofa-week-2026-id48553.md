<p>Dear Slicer Community,</p>
<p>As many of you know, the 3D Slicer and SOFA communities have been working together on the <a href="https://github.com/Slicer/SlicerSOFA" rel="noopener nofollow ugc">SlicerSOFA</a> extension, which brings the simulation capabilities of the <a href="https://www.sofa-framework.org/" rel="noopener nofollow ugc">SOFA Framework</a> directly into Slicer workflows. SOFA is an open-source framework for interactive mechanical simulation, with a strong focus on biomechanics and robotics, and SlicerSOFA keeps opening up opportunities for collaboration between both communities. (SlicerSOFA received the Public Prize of the SOFA open-source awards in 2024!)</p>
<p>To foster these collaborations, the SOFA community organizes its annual event, <a href="https://www.sofa-framework.org/sofa-events/sofa-week-2026/" rel="noopener nofollow ugc"><strong>SOFA Week 2026</strong></a>, which will take place in <strong>Lille, France, from November 23rd to 27th</strong>, following the same format as last year:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Monday, Nov 23</strong>: Training sessions to get started efficiently with SOFA (introductory track, plus an advanced soft-robotics track).</li>
<li><strong>Tuesday, Nov 24</strong>: <a href="https://www.sofa-framework.org/sofa-events/sofa-week-2026/#Symposium" rel="noopener nofollow ugc"><strong>SOFA Symposium</strong></a>, a one-day conference bringing together research teams and companies involved in the SOFA open-source project. About 15 presentations are expected on robotics, biomechanics, medical imaging and coupling AI with simulation, together with live demos from <a href="https://www.sofa-framework.org/sofa-events/sofa-week-2026/#Participants" rel="noopener nofollow ugc">industrial exhibitors</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Wednesday–Friday, Nov 25–27</strong>: Technical Committee meetings and technical sessions, open to everyone.</li>
</ul>
<p>As in previous editions, the week also includes the <strong>SOFA open-source awards</strong>, which reward open-source contributions to SOFA. If you are using SOFA together with Slicer, this is a great venue to present your work. The deadline for presentation and award submissions is <strong>November 6th, 2026</strong>.</p>
<p>👉 <a href="https://www.sofa-framework.org/sofa-events/sofa-week-2026/registration/" rel="noopener nofollow ugc"><strong>Registration</strong></a> for the Symposium is <strong>free but mandatory</strong>, and <strong>remote access</strong> is available upon registration if you cannot make it to Lille.</p>
<p>Please feel free to share this invitation with your team, colleagues and partners. Looking forward to seeing many of you there, in person or online!</p>
<p>Open-sourcely yours,<br>
The SOFA Development Team</p>
