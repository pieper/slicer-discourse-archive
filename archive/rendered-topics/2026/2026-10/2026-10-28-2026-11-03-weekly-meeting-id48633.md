## Post #3 by @Thibault_Pelletier (2026-10-29 13:22 UTC)

<p>Since it was discussed during a previous weekly, I looked a bit into QWebEngineView alternatives and the solution would be in Qt itself with the <a href="https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qwebview.html" rel="noopener nofollow ugc">QWebView</a> component (Qt 6.11+).</p>
<p>Behavior will change depending on the platform:</p>
<ul>
<li>Windows: delegates to WebView2</li>
<li>MacOs: delegates to system WKWebView</li>
<li>Linux: Still requires QtWebEngineView + Chromium</li>
</ul>
<p>Linux systems don’t seem to provide yet native browser embedding, so there wouldn’t be a clean solution for Linux at the moment.</p>
<p>FYI: <a class="mention" href="/u/pieper">@pieper</a> &amp; <a class="mention" href="/u/lassoan">@lassoan</a></p>
