## Post #1 by @doggydoc (2026-10-13 18:49 UTC)

<h2><a name="p-136351-doggybone-veterinary-corrective-osteotomy-tool-1" class="anchor" href="#p-136351-doggybone-veterinary-corrective-osteotomy-tool-1" aria-label="Heading link"></a><strong>DoggyBone – Veterinary Corrective Osteotomy Tool</strong></h2>
<p>Hi everyone,</p>
<p>first of all, I would like to thank the Slicer community for providing such a powerful platform and for all the knowledge and support available through this forum.</p>
<p>I would like to introduce <strong>DoggyBone</strong>, a veterinary 3D Slicer module intended for the planning of <strong>corrective osteotomies of the femur and tibia in dogs</strong>.</p>
<p>The project is based on an original 3D Slicer module developed by <strong>Dr. Juliette Burg at LMU Munich</strong> as part of her doctoral research. Her original work provided tools for the measurement and analysis of femoral and tibial axes.</p>
<p>Building on this foundation, I have modified and expanded the module considerably. In particular, I have worked on:</p>
<ul>
<li>adapting and improving the <strong>user interface and workflow</strong>,</li>
<li>extending the original bone-axis measurements into an <strong>interactive corrective osteotomy planning workflow</strong>,</li>
<li>implementing tools for planning corrections of the <strong>femur and tibia</strong>, and</li>
<li>adding functionality for the <strong>generation of patient-specific surgical guides</strong> based on the planned correction.</li>
</ul>
<p>The additional programming and development were carried out by me <strong>with the assistance of Claude</strong>, particularly for implementing and refining the Python code.</p>
<p>The module is currently still under development, and I am sure there are many aspects that could be improved—both from a programming perspective and regarding integration into the usual 3D Slicer workflow.</p>
<p>I would therefore be very grateful for <strong>feedback, suggestions, criticism, or technical input from experienced Slicer developers and users</strong>. In particular, I would appreciate advice regarding code structure, robustness, UI design, and the implementation of the osteotomy and patient-specific guide workflow.</p>
<p>My aim is to develop DoggyBone into a practical and reliable open tool for <strong>veterinary orthopedic planning</strong>, while keeping it well integrated with the philosophy and existing tools of 3D Slicer.</p>
<p>Thank you very much in advance for taking the time to have a look at the project and for any feedback you may have.</p>
<p>Best regards,<br>
<strong>Matthias</strong></p>
