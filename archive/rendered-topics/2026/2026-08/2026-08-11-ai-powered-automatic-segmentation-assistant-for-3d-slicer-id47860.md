## Post #1 by @AIOmarRehan (2026-08-11 14:03 UTC)

<p>Hi everyone,</p>
<p>I’m interested in contributing to 3D Slicer by developing an <strong>AI-Powered Automatic Segmentation Assistant</strong>.</p>
<p>The idea is to provide a user-friendly workflow for applying modern AI segmentation models to medical images and automatically creating/editing Slicer segmentations.</p>
<p>I’m considering integration with existing approaches such as <strong>MONAI, nnU-Net, and other medical segmentation models</strong>, while making the design modular so additional models can be added later.</p>
<p>Before starting implementation, I’d appreciate feedback from the Slicer community on whether this would be useful, whether similar functionality already exists, and what architecture/workflow would be most appropriate for an upstream contribution.</p>
<p>I’m particularly interested in suggestions regarding integration with <strong>Segment Editor</strong> and existing Slicer AI/segmentation infrastructure.</p>
