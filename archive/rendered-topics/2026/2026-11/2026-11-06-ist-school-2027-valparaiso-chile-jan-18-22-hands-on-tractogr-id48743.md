## Post #1 by @Graham_Little (2026-11-06 01:56 UTC)

<p>Dear colleagues,</p>
<p>Registration is now open for the IST School - Valparaíso 2027, a five-day hands-on tractography workshop run by the International Society for Tractography. Last year’s School filled quickly, so we encourage early registration.</p>
<ul>
<li>Dates: January 18-22, 2027</li>
<li>Location: University of Valparaíso, Valparaíso, Chile</li>
<li>Website and registration: <code>event.fourwaves.com/istschool2027</code></li>
<li>Fees: USD 500 (full), USD 300 (students). Fees include registration, lunch each day and coffee breaks.</li>
<li>Registration closes: December 31, 2026 (space is limited)</li>
</ul>
<p>Experts lead attendees through the complete tractography workflow: preprocessing and quality control of dMRI, advanced modelling (microstructure, NODDI, DKI, fixel-based analysis), deterministic and probabilistic tractography, bundle segmentation, tractometry and structural connectomics. The format is bootcamp-style, pairing lectures with guided hands-on sessions and project consultation, and it suits users of MRtrix3, FSL, FreeSurfer, ANTs, Scilpy, DIPY and other major packages.</p>
<p>The School is designed for PhD students, postdoctoral fellows and clinical scientists who are new to the practical side of dMRI, and for researchers in neuroscience, psychology, radiology and related fields who want applied tractography skills.</p>
<p>Instructors: Pamela Guevara (Universidad de Concepción, Chile), François Rheault (Université de Sherbrooke, Canada), Chiara Maffei (MGH &amp; Harvard Medical School, USA), Alessandro Daducci (University of Verona, Italy), Alexander Leemans (UMC Utrecht, the Netherlands), Luis Concha (UNAM, Mexico) and Alonso Ramírez (CIMAT, Mexico).</p>
<p>New this year: the IST School is partnering with the Latin American School in Computational Neuroscience (LACONEU, <code>laconeu.cl</code>). IST School registrants have the chance to be selected to attend LACONEU as well.</p>
<p>If you need an official invitation letter or attendance confirmation, please email <a href="mailto:info@tractography.io">info@tractography.io</a>.</p>
<p>Best regards,<br>
The IST School 2027 Organizing Committee<br>
International Society for Tractography</p>
