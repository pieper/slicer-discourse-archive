## Post #1 by @bywbilly (2026-09-01 16:12 UTC)

<p>AI-powered segmentation for Spine/Pelvis (CT) and Aorta–Iliac–Femoral Arteries (CTA).</p>
<p>Upload CT volumes directly from 3D Slicer to the DRAI server and receive high-quality, multi-label 3D segmentation masks and models within minutes. Our models are designed to handle complex cases, including bypass grafts, metal implants, extreme scoliosis, and challenging pathology.</p>
<p>For Spine/Pelvis CT, the extension generates individual segmentation masks for each vertebra, as well as the pelvis and sacrum. For CTA, it segments the aorta, iliac arteries, femoral arteries, and their associated vascular structures.</p>
