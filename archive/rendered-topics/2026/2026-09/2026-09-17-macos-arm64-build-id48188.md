## Post #2 by @pieper (2026-09-17 19:10 UTC)

<p>It’s not a coincidence - the x86 builds need to go through Rosetta and it’s very slow (happens serially as they are loaded).  I looked to see if we could do some kind of preprocess to do the do it in parallel but really just putting our effort into distributing the arm64 builds is a better use of time.  It’s a matter of updating the download servers and getting the extension builds in place.  Stay tuned.</p>
