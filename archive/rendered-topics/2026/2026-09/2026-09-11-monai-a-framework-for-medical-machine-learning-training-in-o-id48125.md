<p>This course is designed for developers who want to get familiar with the MONAI framework. MONAI is a PyTorch-based framework designed for deep learning in healthcare imaging. Registration and details at at <a href="https://www.kitware.eu/monai-training/" rel="noopener nofollow ugc">MONAI Training - Kitware Europe</a></p>
